- **Meaning:** bread; loaf
- **Word type:** noun
- **Article:** het (see [[De and het]])
- **Plural:** broden

## Examples
- Ik eet brood. — *I eat bread.*
- Het brood is lekker. — *The bread is tasty.*

## Pronunciation
- /broːt/ - long *oo*; final *d* is pronounced as *t*
