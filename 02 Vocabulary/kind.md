- **Meaning:** child
- **Word type:** noun
- **Article:** het (see [[De and het]])
- **Plural:** kinderen (irregular)

## Examples
- Het kind drinkt [[sap]]. — *The child drinks juice.*
- De kinderen eten [[brood]]. — *The children eat bread.*

## Pronunciation
- /kɪnt/ - short *i*; final *d* is pronounced as *t*
