- **Meaning:** apple
- **Word type:** noun
- **Article:** de (see [[De and het]])
- **Plural:** appels

## Examples
- Ik eet een appel. — *I eat an apple.*
- De [[jongen]] eet de appel. — *The boy eats the apple.*

## Pronunciation
- /ˈɑpəl/ - stress on the first syllable; *-el* is a weak "ul"

## Related
