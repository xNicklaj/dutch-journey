- **Meaning:** she; they
- **Word type:** pronoun (unstressed form of *zij*)
- **Note:** the verb tells you which: *ze is* = she is, *ze zijn* = they are

## Examples
- Ze is een [[vrouw]]. — *She is a woman.*
- Ze drinken [[sap]]. — *They drink juice.*

## Pronunciation
- /zə/ - unstressed, like the "e" in "the"

## Related
- [[ik]], [[het]], [[zijn]]
