- **Meaning:** the (neuter nouns); it
- **Word type:** article; pronoun
- **Note:** as an article only with singular het-words; plurals always take *de* (see [[De and het]])

## Examples
- Het [[kind]] drinkt [[sap]]. — *The child drinks juice.*
- Het is lekker. — *It is tasty.*

## Pronunciation
- /ɦət/ - unstressed; often shortened to *'t* /ət/
